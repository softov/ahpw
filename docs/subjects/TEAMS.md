# Teams and projects

The teams and projects a host groups people by. Usage is charged to them, and a user's memberships name them. ahpw shows them only when the host offers them.

## Where it is

The **Teams** icon in the lower part of the activity bar opens the **Teams** list in the left sidebar. The icon is there only when the host offers users, teams, projects or roles.

<Shot id="teams-list" alt="The Teams list with its Teams and Projects groups" />

The list has two groups, **Teams** and **Projects**. Each row shows the title, or the id when there is none, and its id and member count under it. Type in "Filter teams and projects" to narrow the list.

| Action | |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | A menu with **New team** and **New project** |
| **Reload** | Read the list from the host again |
| **Hosts** | Pick which hosts the list shows, when the window shows several hosts |

Each row's menu has **Open** and **Delete**. The host refuses to delete a team while anybody still belongs to it, or a project while anybody still works on it.

The palette has **New team**, **New project** and **Show teams**.

## Change a team or project

**Open** a row, or pick **New team** or **New project**.

<Shot id="teams-page" alt="A team page with its title and members" />

- **Id**: on a new one only. Memberships are written with it, and it cannot be changed later.
- **Title**: what is shown instead of the id. Empty shows the id.

**Create team**, **Create project** or **Save** writes it. **Cancel** and **Discard changes** drop your edits, and **Delete** removes it.

## Members

The **Members** section shows who belongs, worked out from each user's memberships. It needs the host to offer users.

- On a team: everybody whose memberships name the team, every project of it, or one project through it.
- On a project: everybody who works on it through a team, or through every project of a team.

A member's name opens their user page in [Users and roles](USERS.md). Their chips show how they belong, and the primary chip is lit when this is their primary.

**Add a member** adds a person, then asks **Which projects** on a team or **Which team** on a project. ✕ takes a person out. Adding and removing writes that user's record, so it needs the host to let you change users.
