ahpd's key plans give a minted key a scope, a team in place of a person, limits and meta entries.
The keys form can take each one at mint and show each one in the list.
The grant picker can group grants by subject, so a proxy-only key is two clicks.
A `minute` limit needs a `policies/period/minute` string in the catalog, or the policy and key forms show the bare key.
